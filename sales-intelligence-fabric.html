Case Study | Sales Intelligence

Transforming sales intelligence with Microsoft Fabric Data Agents

How AI Edge helped a heavy equipment dealer establish a governed foundation for next-best-action and next-best-offer recommendations.

Microsoft Fabric Data Agents Sales Intelligence Governance

Customer Challenge

Moving from periodic analysis to intelligent recommendations

The customer had already invested in predictive analytics that generated sales recommendations from a limited set of business indicators. The process remained largely manual, requiring sales leaders to review insights, coach representatives, and prioritize customer engagement through periodic analysis and reporting.

The goal was to create a scalable AI-enabled analytics foundation that could prioritize opportunities, surface actionable insights, incorporate multiple business signals, reduce spreadsheet-driven decision-making, and support future AI-assisted sales coaching.

AI Edge Approach

Business questions first, technology second

01 | Business question engineering

Stakeholders formalized the decisions the solution needed to support, including which customers to prioritize, which opportunities had the highest conversion probability, and what action to take next.

02 | Data and semantic readiness

The team assessed source systems, Fabric readiness, semantic models, KPI definitions, business terminology, relationships, and security requirements.

03 | Fabric Data Agent design

The architecture used governed enterprise data and Fabric semantic models to support role-aware, grounded, explainable recommendations using existing business logic.

04 | Security and governance

The access strategy covered workspaces, semantic models, source connectivity, RBAC alignment, security-trimmed responses, and Microsoft 365 integration readiness.

Architecture Direction

A governed analytics layer with a path to orchestration

The design established a clear separation of responsibilities. Microsoft Fabric Data Agents provided the analytics layer for data access, semantic understanding, KPI interpretation, recommendation generation, and business grounding.

Azure AI Foundry was identified as the future orchestration layer for scheduling, multi-agent coordination, workflow management, and user-specific execution. This separation created a scalable foundation while maintaining clear ownership boundaries.

Signals considered

  • Historical purchase behavior
  • Service history
  • Machine telemetry
  • Promotional participation
  • Geographic and environmental signals
  • Existing predictive analytics outputs

Technical capabilities

  • Fabric Data Agents
  • Semantic model grounding
  • Lakehouse and Warehouse connectivity
  • Security-trimmed responses
  • KPI-aware agent instructions
  • Business terminology and ontology definition

Expected Business Value

A path toward AI-assisted sales decision support

Improved prioritization

Help sales teams focus on the highest-value customer opportunities.

Faster opportunity discovery

Surface relevant signals and recommendations closer to the moment of action.

Less manual analysis

Reduce dependence on periodic reviews and spreadsheet-driven workflows.

More consistent coaching

Support sales leadership with a shared, governed view of customer signals.

Higher analytics adoption

Make enterprise analytics more accessible through natural language interaction.

Reusable AI foundation

Create a path toward future Copilot Studio and multi-agent experiences.

Benefits described here are expected outcomes from the designed capability and should not be interpreted as independently verified production results.
